02

The 4d8 procedure

  1. Roll 4d8 once. No rerolls.
  2. Slot any three dice into Palette, Setting and Tone.
  3. Drop the fourth onto any Dial as the Undertow.

The Undertow · 4th die friction

On Palette Divide

Split class, zone, or day/night (Chrome spires, Rust slums).

On Setting Layer

Stack or collide play spaces (Modern streets, Mythic spirits).

On Tone Counter-Beat

Contrast front vs. stakes, or wilds vs. home (Peril wilds, Cozy camp).

Matched Pair becomes Deep Note

Undertow lands on its own number? Double down on that Dial's extreme; zero dilution.

4-of-a-Kind (N-N-N-N) becomes Pure Archetype

Skip the Undertow; run that row straight at max volume.

04

World chemistry

Pitch paints the cover; chemistry drives play. Regulate four DOSE needs—Chemically (visceral) or Mystically (divine).

  • D Dopamine

    Transactional

    Reward, ambition, scrip, leverage.

  • O Oxytocin

    Relational

    Trust, kinship, belonging, safe harbor.

  • S Serotonin

    Positional

    Dignity, pride, community credit.

  • E Endorphins

    Physical

    Thrill, hunger, pleasure, relief.

Step 1: Flood & Starve

  1. Flood (Palette + Setting): 1–2 cheap, abundant DOSE needs (Neon Future: Endorphins, Dopamine).
  2. Starve (Tone): Rationed or corrupted DOSE need (Punk: Serotonin, Oxytocin).

Core loop: Hustle the Flood to survive; risk everything for the Starve.

06

Opinionated Cortex harness

Compile any 4d8 pitch into fast Cortex Prime on a clean 3-Prime-Set sheet.

Openings (Hitches)

Set 1s aside (not Total/Impact). Foes—or PCs with 1 Chip—seize one for d6 Fallout, a step-up, or an edge. GM pays 1 Chip to seize yours.

Three conflict speeds

01 Roll · single test

One toss vs. GM (2d6 + traits, or Doom Pool). High Total wins; instigator wins ties.

02 Row · escalating contest

Beat the lead Total to take control, or Concede on your terms (+1 Chip). Failing inflicts winner's Impact as Fallout.

03 Romp · multi-die challenge

Roll vs. central Romp Pool (4d8). Win + Impact > Romp die drops it; ≤ steps down smallest (d4 gone). Romp acts last.

Four house cuts

  • Full-Force Hits: Never compare Impact vs. loser's Effect Die. Win Total, land full Impact.
  • Real Fallout Only: Starts at d6 (no d4 Chip-farming). Past d12 drops out of frame.
  • In-Pool Resources: Roll inside pool to keep +1 Total die, then expend until recovery.
  • Side Chars (Session 0): Shared Diamonds hold 2–3 d8 traits; swap up to 2 and absorb fitting Fallout.

Prime Set 1: Distinctions

Write three d8 Distinctions titled after your world's Identity Prompts (Texture · Place · Hook).

Universal Hinder rule: Roll any Distinction as d4 instead of d8 to gain 1 Chip.

07

Cortex Dial Compiler

Match three Dials for Engine Mod, Prime Set 2 and Prime Set 3. Undertow (4th die): gain 1 shared d8 Side Char / Special, OR apply its Engine Mod in contrast scenes.

1 · Palette feeds Engine Mod

  1. 1

    Prismatic (Bleed): Uncanny frames: 1s & 2s = Openings; 1 Chip renames Highlight or Fallout.

  2. 2

    Neon (Burnout): Highlights expire in 1 roll (1 Chip sustains). Lean In: roll own Fallout, then step up.

  3. 3

    Chrome (Spec Sheet): Two d8 Signature Assets (Gear Limit: shut down for frame → +1 Chip).

  4. 4

    Dark (Doom Pool): Escalating d8, d6, d4 GM pool replaces static difficulty; grows on Openings, buys twists/Romps.

  5. 5

    Sepia (Living Serial): Log Session Titles; 1/session invoke for free Chip or Flashback; cross off to advance.

  6. 6

    Bright (Open Table): Zero GM Bank (GM spends go to PCs) + allies share Highlights.

  7. 7

    Pastel (Gentle Stakes): Fallout steps down in quiet scenes; double GM 1s = auto-success or step-down.

  8. 8

    Rust (Scarcity): Two 2d6 Salvage dice (+1 Total die, expend). Stricken: Fallout > trait keeps 1 Total die.

2 · Setting feeds Prime Set 2

  1. 1

    Threshold — Arenas (d10, d8, d6): Anchor, Transit, Beyond.

  2. 2

    Future — Roles (d10, 2×d8, d6, d4): Hacker, Fixer, Operator, Tech, Face.

  3. 3

    Space — Arenas (d10, d8, d6): Void, Station, Worlds + d8 Ship (Engines, Frame, Systems).

  4. 4

    Modern — Roles (d10, d8, d6): Mouth, Mind, Muscle.

  5. 5

    Industrial — Guilds (d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, 2×d4): Machinist, Saboteur, Alchemist, Bruiser, Broker, Medic, Scout, Orator.

  6. 6

    Mythic — Attributes (d10, d8, d6: Might, Grace, Lore) + Calling Special (d6 Pressure die).

  7. 7

    Hearth — Crafts (d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, 2×d4: Mend, Cook, Grow, Travel, Parley, Notice, Tinker, Ward) + two d6 Specialties.

  8. 8

    Primal — Arenas (d10, d8, d6: Camp, Wilds, Lair) + d8 Beast/Pack or Trophy.

3 · Tone feeds Prime Set 3

  1. 1

    Weird — Tether Drives (d10, d8, 2×d6: Reward, Bond, Pride, Thrill): Anchor statements; Question to triple die, step down & rewrite.

  2. 2

    Punk — Bloc Stances (d10, d8, d6 across 3 Factions): Faction stances; Question to triple die & fracture allegiance.

  3. 3

    Adventure — Horizon Values (d10, 2×d8, 2×d6, d4: Daring, Cleverness, Crew, Wonder, Fortune, Duty) + 3 Goals (d6, d8, d10).

  4. 4

    Noir — Compromised Drives (d10, d8, d6, d4: Ambition, Trust, Dignity, Vice): Statements fracture as the case corrupts.

  5. 5

    Pulp — Action Modes (d10, d8, d6: Solo, Duo, Team) + Hero Dice (win by 5+ to bank foe's top die).

  6. 6

    Heroic — Oath Values (d10, d8, 2×d6: Justice, Mercy, Courage, Truth) + Moral Support (step down yours to step up an ally's).

  7. 7

    Cozy — Bonds (d10, 2×d8, rest d6 to PCs/neighbors) + Team Up (lend Bond die risk-free).

  8. 8

    Peril — Nerve (d10, d8, d6: Grit, Focus, Trust) + Trauma (Fallout past d12 leaves d6 Trauma, needs recovery).
