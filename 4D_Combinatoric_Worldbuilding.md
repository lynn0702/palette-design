## PAGE 3 — 03 · THE UNDERTOW

Your fourth `d8` grinds friction into whichever Dial it touches. Place it beneath that Dial's divider line to pick its mode.

- **On Palette · Divide:** Split class, zone, or day and night: _Chrome spires, Rust slums_.
- **On Setting · Layer:** Stack or collide play spaces: _Modern streets, Mythic spirits_.
- **On Tone · Counter:** Contrast front vs. stakes, or wilds vs. home: _wild Perils, Cozy camp_.

## PAGE 7 — 07 · CROSS THE WIRES (PASS 2–4)

### Pass 2: Cross the Wires

- **Left · Rumor:** Draw a line to your left neighbor. State something you've heard about them. They name the real truth.
- **Right · Bond:** Draw a shared Box between you and your right neighbor. Name a shared tie or asset you both rely on: _Ride, Hideout, Workshop, Beast, Patron_.

- **Edge:** Tag your Character Circle with what you're best at in this Setting (your top role, skill, or turf).
- **Fact:** Drop a Diamond onto the canvas for a world truth, Faction, Neighbor, Belief, or Goal pulling at you. Wire your Character to it.

### Pass 4: Event Triangle

GM drops a central inverted Event Triangle where the _Undertow_ bites and wires it to 2+ nodes on the map. That trouble is live right now. Start _in medias res_.

A **Cortex Prime** baseplate to snap your `4d8` pitch and Spaghetti Map to.

### Snap Your Map to Your Sheet

- **Pass 1 (Character Circle):** Your 3 Dial prompt answers become your three `d8` Distinctions (**Prime Set 1**).
- **Pass 2 (Rumor & Bond):** Shared Bond Boxes become `d8` Supports. Rumors seed Relationship or Value Statements.
- **Pass 3 (Edge & Fact):** Your Edge sets your top `d10` in **Prime Set 2** (Page 13). Your Fact Diamond sets your top `d10` or Goal in **Prime Set 3** (Page 14).
- **Pass 4 (Event):** The inverted Event Triangle is your opening scene's opposition or Romp Pool.

### Baseline Pool Equation · + Assets, Resources, and Foe Fallout

- **Build Your Pool:** `[1 · DISTINCTION d8 / d4]` + `[2 · SETTING Prime 2]` + `[3 · TONE Prime 3]`
- **Read the Roll:** `[1s: OPENINGS]` · `[SUM 2: TOTAL]` · `[1 DIE: IMPACT d6–d12]`

## PAGE 10 — 10 · OPENINGS & CONFLICT SPEEDS

### Openings

Set rolled `1`s aside. They never count toward Total or Impact. Foes seize your Openings, handing you `1 PP`, to inflict `d6` Fallout, step up Fallout, or claim an edge. Spend `1 PP` to seize GM Openings. Extra `1`s step up the die by +1 each.

### Three Conflict Speeds

- **01 · Roll · single test:** One toss vs. GM difficulty: `2dX` + scene traits, or the Doom Pool. High Total wins. Instigator wins ties.
- **02 · Row · escalating contest:** Beat the lead Total to take control, or **Concede** on your terms for `+1 PP` when you could roll again. Failing inflicts the winner's Impact as Fallout.
- **03 · Romp · multi-die challenge:** Roll vs. a central `YdX` Romp Pool. Win with Impact `>` a Romp die to drop it, or `≤` to step down the smallest. `d4` drops out. Romps act last.

## PAGE 11 — 11 · HOUSE CUTS & DISTINCTIONS

### Four House Cuts

- **Full-Force Hits:** Never step down your Impact against a loser's bigger die. Win the Total, land full Impact.
- **Real Fallout Only:** Starts at `d6` minimum. No `d4` PP-farming. Steps down by one in quiet recovery scenes. Stepping past `d12` drops you out of the scene.
- **In-Pool Resources:** Roll a Resource die inside your pool to keep `+1` Total die, then expend until recovery.
- **Supports:** Shared use Allies, Vehicles, Locations, etc. Starting with 1–3 traits; mix and swap their traits with yours in pools; let them absorb fitting Fallout.

### Prime Set 1: Distinctions

Write three `d8` Distinctions titled after your world's Page 6 Identity Prompts.

> **HINDER**
> Roll any Distinction as `d4` instead of `d8` to gain `1 PP`.

**Undertow in Cortex:** Players gain 1 related `d8` Support or Special. GMs run its Engine Mod in contrast scenes.

## PAGE 12 — 12 · PALETTE → ENGINE MOD

1. **Prismatic · Bleed** — When the uncanny bleeds into a scene, `1`s & `2`s both count as Openings. Spend `1 PP` to rename any Asset or Fallout in play.
8. **Sepia · Living Serial** — Log Session Titles as Session Records. Once per session, invoke one for a free `1 PP` effect or Flashback. Cross it off between sessions to step up a trait or add a `d8` Signature Asset.
9. **Neon · Burnout** — Created Assets last 1 roll. Spend `1 PP` to sustain for the scene. **Lean In:** roll your own Fallout, then step it up.
10. **Pastel · Gentle Stakes** — PC Fallout lands at `d6`, or steps up existing Fallout by one. It steps down automatically in quiet scenes.
11. **Bright · Open Table** — **No GM Bank:** GM `PP` spends go straight to targeted PCs. Allies share created Assets for free.
12. **Dark · Doom Pool** — An escalating `d8, d6, d4` GM pool replaces difficulty dice pairs. It grows on player Openings and spends dice for twists or Romps.
13. **Chrome · Spec Sheet** — Gain two `d8` Signature Assets with _Gear Limit_: shut one down for the scene to gain `1 PP`.
14. **Rust · Scarcity** — Gain `2d6` Salvage Resources. **Stricken:** Any `d10+` Fallout restricts you to keeping 1 Total die.

## PAGE 13 — 13 · SETTING → PRIME SET 2

1. **Liminal** — Attributes `d10, d8, d6`: _Pulse, Pattern, Poise_ + one `d8` Ability with 1 Special & Limit: shut down for the scene for `+1 PP`.
8. **Industrial** — Skills `d10, 2×d8, 3×d6`, rest `d4`: _Brawl, Craft, Delve, Doctor, Prowl, Rig, Survey, Sway_.
9. **Future** — Roles `d10, 2×d8, d6, d4`: _Hacker, Fixer, Operator, Tech, Face_.
10. **Hearth** — Skills `d10, 2×d8, 3×d6`, rest `d4`: _Cook, Grow, Mend, Notice, Parley, Tinker, Travel, Ward_ + two `d6` Specialties.
11. **Mythic** — Attributes `d10, d8, d6`: _Might, Grace, Lore_ + Power Set of two `d8` mythic Powers & a Limit.
12. **Modern** — Roles `d10, d8, d6`: _Mouth, Mind, Muscle_ + one unrated narrative Talent.
13. **Space** — Affiliations: Arenas `d10, d8, d6`: _Void, Station, Surface_ + shared `d8` Vehicle rated in _Engines, Frame, Systems_.
14. **Primal** — Affiliations: Arenas `d10, d8, d6`: _Camp, Wilds, Lair_ + `d8` Beast Support or Signature Asset.

## PAGE 14 — 14 · TONE → PRIME SET 3

1. **Weird** — Values `d10, d8, 2×d6`: _Order, Truth, Wonder, Self_ with Statements. **Question** a belief to triple its die for one roll, then step down & rewrite.
8. **Pulp** — Affiliations `d10, d8, d6`: _Solo, Duo, Team_ + **Hero Dice:** win by 5+ to bank a foe's top-rolling die. Spend `1 PP` later to roll & add to Total.
9. **Punk** — Relationships: Factions `d10, d8, d6` across 3 dominant Blocs with Statements. **Question** your stance to triple its die & burn standing.
10. **Cozy** — Relationships `d10, 2×d8`, rest `d6` toward PCs & neighbors + **Team Up:** lend your Relationship die into an ally's pool risk-free.
11. **Heroic** — Values `d10, d8, 2×d6`: _Courage, Justice, Mercy, Truth_ + **Moral Support:** step down your Value for the session to step up an ally's.
12. **Noir** — Relationships `d10, 2×d8`, rest `d6` toward PCs, informants & suspects with Statements + **GMC Vices:** GMCs oppose using _Greed, Control, Wrath_.
13. **Adventure** — Values `d10, d8, 2×d6, d4`: _Discovery, Duty, Fortune, Glory, Loyalty_ + three Goals at `d6, d8, d10`: roll & cross off in opposition, or check off when achieved to step up a trait.
14. **Peril** — Reserves `d10, d8, 2×d6`: _Stamina, Resolve, Supply, Luck_ + **Usage Dice:** rolling `1–2` on a Reserve steps it down after the roll. `d4` drops out. Rest resets them.
