Opinionated Cortex harness

Turn any 4d8 pitch into fast Cortex Prime on a clean 3-Prime-Set sheet.

Openings

Set rolled 1s aside—they never count toward Total or Impact. Foes seize player Openings, handing that player 1 PP, to inflict d6 Fallout, step up Fallout, or claim an edge; PCs spend 1 PP to seize GM Openings. Extra 1s step up the resulting die by +1 each.

Three conflict speeds

01 Roll · single test

One toss vs. GM difficulty—2d6 + scene traits, or the Doom Pool. High Total wins; instigator wins ties.

02 Row · escalating contest

Beat the lead Total to take control, or Concede on your terms for +1 PP. Failing inflicts the winner's Impact as Fallout.

03 Romp · multi-die challenge

Roll vs. a central 4d8 Romp Pool. Win with Impact > a Romp die to drop it, or ≤ to step down the smallest—d4 drops out. Romp acts last.

Four house cuts

  • Full-Force Hits: Never step down your Impact against a loser's bigger die. Win the Total, land full Impact.
  • Real Fallout Only: Starts at d6 minimum—no d4 PP-farming—and steps down by one in quiet recovery scenes. Stepping past d12 drops you out of the scene.
  • In-Pool Resources: Roll a Resource die inside your pool to keep +1 Total die, then expend until recovery.
  • Side Chars: Shared Session 0 Anchors hold 2–3 d8 traits; swap in up to 2 and let them absorb fitting Fallout.

Prime Set 1: Distinctions

Write three d8 Distinctions titled after your world's Identity Prompts.

Universal Hinder rule: Roll any Distinction as d4 instead of d8 to gain 1 PP.

Cortex Sheet & Mods

Undertow: gain 1 shared d8 Side Char or Special, or run its Engine Mod in contrast scenes.

1 · Palette feeds Engine Mod

  1. 1

    Prismatic · Bleed — When the uncanny bleeds into a scene, 1s & 2s both count as Openings; spend 1 PP to rename any Asset or Fallout in play.

  2. 2

    Neon · Burnout — Created Assets last 1 roll; spend 1 PP to sustain for the scene. Lean In: roll your own Fallout, then step it up.

  3. 3

    Chrome · Spec Sheet — Gain two d8 Signature Assets with Gear Limit: shut one down for the scene to gain 1 PP.

  4. 4

    Dark · Doom Pool — An escalating d8, d6, d4 GM pool replaces static difficulty, growing on player Openings and spending dice for twists or Romps.

  5. 5

    Sepia · Living Serial — Log Session Titles; 1/session invoke one for a free 1 PP effect or Flashback, or cross it off between sessions to step up a trait or add a d8 Signature Asset.

  6. 6

    Bright · Open Table — No GM Bank: GM PP spends go straight to targeted PCs, and allies share created Assets for free.

  7. 7

    Pastel · Gentle Stakes — PC Fallout lands at d6—or steps up existing Fallout by one—and steps down automatically in quiet scenes.

  8. 8

    Rust · Scarcity — Gain 2d6 Salvage Resources. Stricken: Fallout larger than its attached trait restricts you to keeping 1 Total die.

2 · Setting feeds Prime Set 2

  1. 1

    Liminal — Attributes d10, d8, d6: Pulse, Pattern, Poise + one d8 Ability with 1 Special & Limit: shut down for the scene for +1 PP.

  2. 2

    Future — Roles d10, 2×d8, d6, d4: Hacker, Fixer, Operator, Tech, Face.

  3. 3

    Space — Affiliations: Arenas d10, d8, d6: Void, Station, Surface + shared d8 Vehicle rated in Engines, Frame, Systems.

  4. 4

    Modern — Roles d10, d8, d6: Mouth, Mind, Muscle + one unrated narrative Talent.

  5. 5

    Industrial — Skills d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, rest d4: Brawl, Craft, Delve, Doctor, Prowl, Rig, Survey, Sway.

  6. 6

    Mythic — Attributes d10, d8, d6: Might, Grace, Lore + Power Set of two d8 mythic Powers & a Limit.

  7. 7

    Hearth — Skills d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, rest d4: Cook, Grow, Mend, Notice, Parley, Tinker, Travel, Ward + two d6 Specialties.

  8. 8

    Primal — Affiliations: Arenas d10, d8, d6: Camp, Wilds, Lair + d8 Beast Side Char or Signature Asset.

3 · Tone feeds Prime Set 3

  1. 1

    Weird — Values d10, d8, 2×d6: Order, Truth, Wonder, Self with Statements. Question a belief to triple its die for one roll, then step down & rewrite.

  2. 2

    Punk — Relationships: Factions d10, d8, d6 across 3 dominant Blocs with Statements. Question your stance to triple its die & burn standing.

  3. 3

    Adventure — Values d10, d8, 2×d6, d4: Discovery, Duty, Fortune, Glory, Loyalty + three Goals at d6, d8, d10: roll & cross off in opposition, or check off when achieved to step up a trait.

  4. 4

    Noir — Relationships d10, 2×d8, rest d6 toward PCs, informants & suspects with Statements + GMC Vices: GMCs oppose using Greed, Control, Wrath.

  5. 5

    Pulp — Affiliations d10, d8, d6: Solo, Duo, Team + Hero Dice: win by 5+ to bank a foe's top-rolling die; spend 1 PP later to roll & add to Total.

  6. 6

    Heroic — Values d10, d8, 2×d6: Courage, Justice, Mercy, Truth + Moral Support: step down your Value for the session to step up an ally's.

  7. 7

    Cozy — Relationships d10, 2×d8, rest d6 toward PCs & neighbors + Team Up: lend your Relationship die into an ally's pool risk-free.

  8. 8

    Peril — Reserves d10, d8, 2×d6: Stamina, Resolve, Supply, Luck + Usage Dice: rolling 1–2 on a Reserve steps it down after the roll; d4 drops out, and rest resets them.
