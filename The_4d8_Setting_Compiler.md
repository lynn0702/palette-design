# 4D COMBINATORIC
## WORLDBUILDING

*Three words sell a world. Four d8s ignite a campaign.*

P1–2: SYSTEM-NEUTRAL ENGINE | P3–4: CORTEX PRIME HARNESS

### Roll a Pitch

* **Roll** `4d8`.
* **Place** three dice into **Palette**, **Setting**, and **Tone**.
* **Grind** the fourth into any Dial as the **Undertow**.

> **Matched Pair → Double Down**
> Undertow lands on its own number? Push that Dial to its extreme.

> **4-of-a-Kind → Pure Archetype**
> Skip the Undertow; run that row straight at max volume.

### THE UNDERTOW

* **On Palette · Divide:** Split class, zone, or day and night: *Chrome spires, Rust slums*.
* **On Setting · Layer:** Stack or collide play spaces: *Modern streets, Mythic spirits*.
* **On Tone · Counter:** Contrast front vs. stakes, or wilds vs. home: *Peril wilds, Cozy camp*.

## PAGE 3 — 03 · OPINIONATED CORTEX HARNESS · *CORE ENGINE*

Turn any `4d8` pitch into fast **Cortex Prime** on a clean 3-Prime-Set sheet.

### BASELINE POOL EQUATION · + ASSETS, RESOURCES, FOE FALLOUT

**[1 · DISTINCTION `d8 / d4`] + [2 · SETTING `Prime 2`] + [3 · TONE `Prime 3`] → [1s: OPENINGS] [SUM 2: TOTAL] [1 DIE SIZE: IMPACT d6–d12]**

### Openings

Set rolled `1`s aside—they never count toward Total or Impact. Foes seize player Openings, handing that player `1 PP`, to inflict `d6` Fallout, step up Fallout, or claim an edge; PCs spend `1 PP` to seize GM Openings. Extra `1`s step up the resulting die by +1 each.

### Three Conflict Speeds

**01 · ROLL · SINGLE TEST**
One toss vs. GM difficulty—`2d6` + scene traits, or the Doom Pool. High Total wins; **instigator wins ties**.

**02 · ROW · ESCALATING CONTEST**
Beat the lead Total to take control, or **Concede** on your terms for `+1 PP`. Failing inflicts the winner's Impact as Fallout.

**03 · ROMP · MULTI-DIE CHALLENGE**
Roll vs. a central `4d8` **Romp Pool**. Win with Impact **>** a Romp die to drop it, or **≤** to step down the smallest—`d4` drops out. Romp acts last.

### Four House Cuts

* **Full-Force Hits:** Never step down your Impact against a loser's bigger die. Win the Total, land full Impact.
* **Real Fallout Only:** Starts at `d6` minimum—no `d4` PP-farming—and steps down by one in quiet recovery scenes. Stepping past `d12` drops you out of the scene.
* **In-Pool Resources:** Roll a Resource die inside your pool to **keep +1 Total die**, then expend until recovery.
* **Side Chars:** Shared Session 0 Anchors hold 2–3 `d8` traits; swap in up to 2 and let them absorb fitting Fallout.

### Prime Set 1: Distinctions

Write **three** `d8` **Distinctions** titled after your world's **Page 2 Identity Prompts**.

> **UNIVERSAL HINDER RULE**
> Roll any Distinction as `d4` instead of `d8` to gain `1 PP`.

## PAGE 4 — Back Cover · 04 · CORTEX SHEET & MODS

**Undertow:** gain 1 shared `d8` Side Char or Special, or run its Engine Mod in contrast scenes.

### 1 · PALETTE → ENGINE MOD

1. **Prismatic · Bleed** — When the uncanny bleeds into a scene, `1`s & `2`s both count as Openings; spend `1 PP` to rename any Asset or Fallout in play.
2. **Neon · Burnout** — Created Assets last 1 roll; spend `1 PP` to sustain for the scene. **Lean In:** roll your own Fallout, then step it up.
3. **Chrome · Spec Sheet** — Gain two `d8` **Signature Assets** with *Gear Limit*: shut one down for the scene to gain `1 PP`.
4. **Dark · Doom Pool** — An escalating `d8, d6, d4` GM pool replaces static difficulty, growing on player Openings and spending dice for twists or Romps.
5. **Sepia · Living Serial** — Log **Session Titles**; 1/session invoke one for a free `1 PP` effect or **Flashback**, or cross it off between sessions to step up a trait or add a `d8` Signature Asset.
6. **Bright · Open Table** — **No GM Bank:** GM `PP` spends go straight to targeted PCs, and allies share created Assets for free.
7. **Pastel · Gentle Stakes** — PC Fallout lands at `d6`—or steps up existing Fallout by one—and steps down automatically in quiet scenes.
8. **Rust · Scarcity** — Gain `2d6` **Salvage Resources**. **Stricken:** Any `d10+` Fallout restricts you to keeping 1 Total die.

### 2 · SETTING → PRIME SET 2

1. **Liminal** — **Attributes** `d10, d8, d6`: *Pulse, Pattern, Poise* + one `d8` **Ability** with 1 Special & Limit: shut down for the scene for `+1 PP`.
2. **Future** — **Roles** `d10, 2×d8, d6, d4`: *Hacker, Fixer, Operator, Tech, Face*.
3. **Space** — **Affiliations: Arenas** `d10, d8, d6`: *Void, Station, Surface* + shared `d8` **Vehicle** rated in *Engines, Frame, Systems*.
4. **Modern** — **Roles** `d10, d8, d6`: *Mouth, Mind, Muscle* + one unrated narrative **Talent**.
5. **Industrial** — **Skills** `d10, 2×d8, 3×d6`, rest `d4`: *Brawl, Craft, Delve, Doctor, Prowl, Rig, Survey, Sway*.
6. **Mythic** — **Attributes** `d10, d8, d6`: *Might, Grace, Lore* + **Power Set** of two `d8` mythic Powers & a Limit.
7. **Hearth** — **Skills** `d10, 2×d8, 3×d6`, rest `d4`: *Cook, Grow, Mend, Notice, Parley, Tinker, Travel, Ward* + two `d6` **Specialties**.
8. **Primal** — **Affiliations: Arenas** `d10, d8, d6`: *Camp, Wilds, Lair* + `d8` **Beast Side Char** or **Signature Asset**.

### 3 · TONE → PRIME SET 3

1. **Weird** — **Values** `d10, d8, 2×d6`: *Order, Truth, Wonder, Self* with **Statements**. **Question** a belief to **triple** its die for one roll, then step down & rewrite.
2. **Punk** — **Relationships: Factions** `d10, d8, d6` across 3 dominant Blocs with **Statements**. **Question** your stance to **triple** its die & burn standing.
3. **Adventure** — **Values** `d10, d8, 2×d6, d4`: *Discovery, Duty, Fortune, Glory, Loyalty* + three **Goals** at `d6, d8, d10`: roll & cross off in opposition, or check off when achieved to step up a trait.
4. **Noir** — **Relationships** `d10, 2×d8`, rest `d6` toward PCs, informants & suspects with **Statements** + **GMC Vices**: GMCs oppose using *Greed, Control, Wrath*.
5. **Pulp** — **Affiliations** `d10, d8, d6`: *Solo, Duo, Team* + **Hero Dice**: win by 5+ to bank a foe's top-rolling die; spend `1 PP` later to roll & add to Total.
6. **Heroic** — **Values** `d10, d8, 2×d6`: *Courage, Justice, Mercy, Truth* + **Moral Support**: step down your Value for the session to step up an ally's.
7. **Cozy** — **Relationships** `d10, 2×d8`, rest `d6` toward PCs & neighbors + **Team Up**: lend your Relationship die into an ally's pool risk-free.
8. **Peril** — **Reserves** `d10, d8, 2×d6`: *Stamina, Resolve, Supply, Luck* + **Usage Dice**: rolling `1–2` on a Reserve steps it down after the roll; `d4` drops out, and rest resets them.
