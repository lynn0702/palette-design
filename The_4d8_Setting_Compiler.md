### The 4d8 Procedure

Roll `4d8` once (no rerolls). Slot 3 into **Palette**, **Setting**, **Tone** (*512 pitches*); drop the 4th as **Undertow**.

> **Matched Pair → Double Down**
> Push that Dial to its extreme.

> **4-of-a-Kind → Pure Archetype**
> Skip Undertow; run row straight at max volume.

### THE UNDERTOW · 4TH DIE

* **Palette** *(Divide)*: Split class, zone, or day/night (*Chrome spires, Rust slums*).
* **Setting** *(Layer)*: Stack or collide play spaces (*Modern streets, Mythic spirits*).
* **Tone** *(Counter-Beat)*: Contrast front vs. stakes, or wilds vs. home (*Peril wilds, Cozy camp*).

## PAGE 3 — 03 · OPINIONATED CORTEX HARNESS · *CORE ENGINE*

Compile any `4d8` pitch into fast **Cortex Prime** on a clean 3-Prime-Set sheet.

### BASELINE POOL EQUATION · + ASSETS, RESOURCES, FOE FALLOUT

**[1 · DISTINCTION `d8 / d4`] + [2 · SETTING `Prime 2`] + [3 · TONE `Prime 3`] → [1s: OPENINGS] [SUM 2: TOTAL] [1 DIE SIZE: IMPACT d6–d12]**

### Openings (Hitches)

Set `1`s aside (not Total/Impact). Foes—or PCs with `1 Chip`—seize one for `d6` Fallout, a step-up, or an edge. GM pays `1 Chip` to seize yours.

### Three Conflict Speeds

**01 · ROLL · SINGLE TEST**
One toss vs. GM (`2d6` + traits, or Doom Pool). High Total wins; **instigator wins ties**.

**02 · ROW · ESCALATING CONTEST**
Beat the lead Total to take control, or **Concede** on your terms (`+1 Chip`). Failing inflicts winner's Impact as Fallout.

**03 · ROMP · MULTI-DIE CHALLENGE**
Roll vs. central **Romp Pool** (`4d8`). Win + Impact **>** Romp die drops it; **≤** steps down smallest (`d4` gone). Romp acts last.

### Four House Cuts

* **Full-Force Hits:** Never compare Impact vs. loser's Effect Die. Win Total, land full Impact.
* **Real Fallout Only:** Starts at `d6` (no `d4` Chip-farming). Past `d12` drops out of frame.
* **In-Pool Resources:** Roll inside pool to **keep +1 Total die**, then expend until recovery.
* **Side Chars** *(Session 0)*: Shared Diamonds hold 2–3 `d8` traits; swap up to 2 and absorb fitting Fallout.

### Prime Set 1: Distinctions

Write **three** `d8` **Distinctions** titled after your world's **Page 2 Identity Prompts** (*Texture · Place · Hook*).

> **UNIVERSAL HINDER RULE**
> Roll any Distinction as `d4` instead of `d8` to gain `1 Chip`.

## PAGE 4 — Back Cover · 04 · CORTEX DIAL COMPILER · *SHEET & MODS*

Match 3 Dials for **Engine Mod**, **Prime Set 2**, **Prime Set 3**. **Undertow (4th Die):** Gain 1 shared `d8` Side Char / Special, OR apply its Engine Mod in contrast scenes.

### 1 · PALETTE → ENGINE MOD

1. **Prismatic** *(Bleed)*: Uncanny frames: `1`s & `2`s = Openings; `1 Chip` renames Highlight or Fallout.
2. **Neon** *(Burnout)*: Highlights expire in 1 roll (`1 Chip` sustains). **Lean In:** roll own Fallout, then step up.
3. **Chrome** *(Spec Sheet)*: Two `d8` **Signature Assets** (*Gear Limit:* shut down for frame → `+1 Chip`).
4. **Dark** *(Doom Pool)*: Escalating `d8, d6, d4` GM pool replaces static difficulty; grows on Openings, buys twists/Romps.
5. **Sepia** *(Living Serial)*: Log **Session Titles**; 1/session invoke for free Chip or **Flashback**; cross off to advance.
6. **Bright** *(Open Table)*: **Zero GM Bank** (GM spends go to PCs) + allies share Highlights.
7. **Pastel** *(Gentle Stakes)*: Fallout steps down in quiet scenes; double GM `1`s = auto-success or step-down.
8. **Rust** *(Scarcity)*: Two `2d6` **Salvage** dice (+1 Total die, expend). **Stricken:** Fallout > trait keeps 1 Total die.

### 2 · SETTING → PRIME SET 2

1. **Threshold** — Arenas (`d10, d8, d6`): *Anchor, Transit, Beyond.*
2. **Future** — Roles (`d10, 2×d8, d6, d4`): *Hacker, Fixer, Operator, Tech, Face.*
3. **Space** — Arenas (`d10, d8, d6`): *Void, Station, Worlds* + `d8` **Ship** (*Engines, Frame, Systems*).
4. **Modern** — Roles (`d10, d8, d6`): *Mouth, Mind, Muscle.*
5. **Industrial** — Guilds (`d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, 2×d4`): *Machinist, Saboteur, Alchemist, Bruiser, Broker, Medic, Scout, Orator.*
6. **Mythic** — Attributes (`d10, d8, d6`: *Might, Grace, Lore*) + **Calling Special** (`d6` Pressure die).
7. **Hearth** — Crafts (`d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, 2×d4`: *Mend, Cook, Grow, Travel, Parley, Notice, Tinker, Ward*) + two `d6` Specialties.
8. **Primal** — Arenas (`d10, d8, d6`: *Camp, Wilds, Lair*) + `d8` **Beast/Pack** or **Trophy**.

### 3 · TONE → PRIME SET 3

1. **Weird** — Tether Drives (`d10, d8, 2×d6`: *Reward, Bond, Pride, Thrill*): Anchor statements; **Question** to **triple** die, step down & rewrite.
2. **Punk** — Bloc Stances (`d10, d8, d6` across 3 Factions): Faction stances; **Question** to **triple** die & fracture allegiance.
3. **Adventure** — Horizon Values (`d10, 2×d8, 2×d6, d4`: *Daring, Cleverness, Crew, Wonder, Fortune, Duty*) + 3 **Goals** (`d6, d8, d10`).
4. **Noir** — Compromised Drives (`d10, d8, d6, d4`: *Ambition, Trust, Dignity, Vice*): Statements fracture as the case corrupts.
5. **Pulp** — Action Modes (`d10, d8, d6`: *Solo, Duo, Team*) + **Hero Dice** (win by 5+ to bank foe's top die).
6. **Heroic** — Oath Values (`d10, d8, 2×d6`: *Justice, Mercy, Courage, Truth*) + **Moral Support** (step down yours to step up an ally's).
7. **Cozy** — Bonds (`d10, 2×d8, rest d6` to PCs/neighbors) + **Team Up** (lend Bond die risk-free).
8. **Peril** — Nerve (`d10, d8, d6`: *Grit, Focus, Trust*) + **Trauma** (Fallout past `d12` leaves `d6` Trauma, needs recovery).
