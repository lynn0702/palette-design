Pass 2: Cross the wires

  • Left · Rumor: Draw a line to your left neighbor. State something you've heard about them. They name the real truth.
  • Right · Bond: Draw a shared Box between you and your right neighbor. Name a shared tie or asset you both rely on: Ride, Hideout, Workshop, Beast, Patron.

Pass 3: Stake your claim

  • Edge: Tag your Character Circle with what you're best at in this Setting (your top role, skill, or turf).
  • Fact: Drop a Diamond onto the canvas for a world truth, Faction, Neighbor, Belief, or Goal pulling at you. Wire your Character to it.

Pass 4: Event triangle

GM drops a central inverted Event Triangle where the Undertow bites and wires it to 2+ nodes on the map. Start in medias res.

House Ruled Cortex

A Cortex Prime baseplate to snap your 4d8 pitch and Spaghetti Map to.

Snap your map to your sheet

  • Pass 1 (Character Circle): Your 3 Dial prompt answers become your three d8 Distinctions (Prime Set 1).
  • Pass 2 (Rumor & Bond): Shared Bond Boxes become d8 Supports. Rumors seed Relationship or Value Statements.
  • Pass 3 (Edge & Fact): Your Edge sets your top d10 in Prime Set 2. Your Fact Diamond sets your top d10 or Goal in Prime Set 3.
  • Pass 4 (Event): The inverted Event Triangle is your opening scene's opposition or Romp Pool.

Openings

Set rolled 1s aside. They never count toward Total or Impact. Foes seize your Openings, handing you 1 PP, to inflict d6 Fallout, step up Fallout, or claim an edge. Spend 1 PP to seize GM Openings. Extra 1s step up the die by +1 each.

Three conflict speeds

01 Roll · single test

One toss vs. GM difficulty: 2dX + scene traits, or the Doom Pool. High Total wins. Instigator wins ties.

02 Row · escalating contest

Beat the lead Total to take control, or Concede on your terms for +1 PP when you could roll again. Failing inflicts the winner's Impact as Fallout.

03 Romp · multi-die challenge

Roll vs. a central YdX Romp Pool. Win with Impact > a Romp die to drop it, or ≤ to step down the smallest. d4 drops out. Romps act last.

Four house cuts

  • Full-Force Hits: Never step down your Impact against a loser's bigger die. Win the Total, land full Impact.
  • Real Fallout Only: Starts at d6 minimum. No d4 PP-farming. Steps down by one in quiet recovery scenes. Stepping past d12 drops you out of the scene.
  • In-Pool Resources: Roll a Resource die inside your pool to keep +1 Total die, then expend until recovery.
  • Supports: Shared use Allies, Vehicles, Locations, etc. Starting with 1–3 traits; mix and swap their traits with yours in pools; let them absorb fitting Fallout.

Prime Set 1: Distinctions

Write three d8 Distinctions titled after your world's Identity Prompts.

Hinder: Roll any Distinction as d4 instead of d8 to gain 1 PP.

Cortex Sheet & Mods

Undertow: Players gain 1 related d8 Support or Special. GMs run its Engine Mod in contrast scenes.

1 · Palette feeds Engine Mod

  1. 1

    Prismatic · Bleed — When the uncanny bleeds into a scene, 1s & 2s both count as Openings. Spend 1 PP to rename any Asset or Fallout in play.

  2. 2

    Sepia · Living Serial — Log Session Titles as Session Records. Once per session, invoke one for a free 1 PP effect or Flashback. Cross it off between sessions to step up a trait or add a d8 Signature Asset.

  3. 3

    Neon · Burnout — Created Assets last 1 roll. Spend 1 PP to sustain for the scene. Lean In: roll your own Fallout, then step it up.

  4. 4

    Pastel · Gentle Stakes — PC Fallout lands at d6, or steps up existing Fallout by one. It steps down automatically in quiet scenes.

  5. 5

    Bright · Open Table — No GM Bank: GM PP spends go straight to targeted PCs. Allies share created Assets for free.

  6. 6

    Dark · Doom Pool — An escalating d8, d6, d4 GM pool replaces difficulty dice pairs. It grows on player Openings and spends dice for twists or Romps.

  7. 7

    Chrome · Spec Sheet — Gain two d8 Signature Assets with Gear Limit: shut one down for the scene to gain 1 PP.

  8. 8

    Rust · Scarcity — Gain 2d6 Salvage Resources. Stricken: Any d10+ Fallout restricts you to keeping 1 Total die.

2 · Setting feeds Prime Set 2

  1. 1

    Liminal — Attributes d10, d8, d6: Pulse, Pattern, Poise + one d8 Ability with 1 Special & Limit: shut down for the scene for +1 PP.

  2. 2

    Industrial — Skills d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, rest d4: Brawl, Craft, Delve, Doctor, Prowl, Rig, Survey, Sway.

  3. 3

    Future — Roles d10, 2×d8, d6, d4: Hacker, Fixer, Operator, Tech, Face.

  4. 4

    Hearth — Skills d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, rest d4: Cook, Grow, Mend, Notice, Parley, Tinker, Travel, Ward + two d6 Specialties.

  5. 5

    Mythic — Attributes d10, d8, d6: Might, Grace, Lore + Power Set of two d8 mythic Powers & a Limit.

  6. 6

    Modern — Roles d10, d8, d6: Mouth, Mind, Muscle + one unrated narrative Talent.

  7. 7

    Space — Affiliations: Arenas d10, d8, d6: Void, Station, Surface + shared d8 Vehicle rated in Engines, Frame, Systems.

  8. 8

    Primal — Affiliations: Arenas d10, d8, d6: Camp, Wilds, Lair + d8 Beast Support or Signature Asset.

3 · Tone feeds Prime Set 3

  1. 1

    Weird — Values d10, d8, 2×d6: Order, Truth, Wonder, Self with Statements. Question a belief to triple its die for one roll, then step down & rewrite.

  2. 2

    Pulp — Affiliations d10, d8, d6: Solo, Duo, Team + Hero Dice: win by 5+ to bank a foe's top-rolling die. Spend 1 PP later to roll & add to Total.

  3. 3

    Punk — Relationships: Factions d10, d8, d6 across 3 dominant Blocs with Statements. Question your stance to triple its die & burn standing.

  4. 4

    Cozy — Relationships d10, 2×d8, rest d6 toward PCs & neighbors + Team Up: lend your Relationship die into an ally's pool risk-free.

  5. 5

    Heroic — Values d10, d8, 2×d6: Courage, Justice, Mercy, Truth + Moral Support: step down your Value for the session to step up an ally's.

  6. 6

    Noir — Relationships d10, 2×d8, rest d6 toward PCs, informants & suspects with Statements + GMC Vices: GMCs oppose using Greed, Control, Wrath.

  7. 7

    Adventure — Values d10, d8, 2×d6, d4: Discovery, Duty, Fortune, Glory, Loyalty + three Goals at d6, d8, d10: roll & cross off in opposition, or check off when achieved to step up a trait.

  8. 8

    Peril — Reserves d10, d8, 2×d6: Stamina, Resolve, Supply, Luck + Usage Dice: rolling 1–2 on a Reserve steps it down after the roll. d4 drops out. Rest resets them.
