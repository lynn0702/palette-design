The 4d8 procedure

  1. Roll 4d8 once. No rerolls.
  2. Slot any three dice into Palette, Setting and Tone.
  3. Drop the fourth onto any Dial as the Undertow.

The Undertow: friction from the fourth die

On Palette Divide

Split class, zone, or day/night (Chrome spires, Rust slums).

On Setting Layer

Stack or collide play spaces (Modern streets, Mythic spirits).

On Tone Counter-Beat

Contrast front vs. stakes, or wilds vs. home (Peril wilds, Cozy camp).

Matched Pair becomes Double Down

Undertow lands on its own number? Push that Dial to its extreme.

4-of-a-Kind becomes Pure Archetype

Skip the Undertow; run that row straight at max volume.

Pass 2: Cross the wires

  • Left (Credit): Declare your left neighbor's public credit—and the messy truth.

Pass 3: Ignition triangle

GM drops a central Triangle (Situation) where the Undertow bites, wired to 2+ nodes. Start in medias res in Scene 1.

Opinionated Cortex harness

Compile any 4d8 pitch into fast Cortex Prime on a clean 3-Prime-Set sheet—unified around Plot Points (PP), Openings, Fallout, Specials, Impact, Assets, Scenes, and Roll / Row / Romp.

Openings

Set rolled 1s aside (never counted toward Total or Impact). Foes seize player Openings—handing that player 1 PP—to inflict d6 Fallout, step up Fallout, or claim an edge; PCs spend 1 PP to seize GM Openings. Extra 1s step up the resulting die by +1 each.

Three conflict speeds

Roll single test

One toss vs. GM (2d6 + scene traits, or Doom Pool). High Total wins; instigator wins ties.

Row escalating contest

Beat the lead Total to take control, or Concede on your terms (+1 PP). Failing inflicts winner's Impact as Fallout.

Romp multi-die challenge

Roll vs. central Romp Pool (4d8). Win + Impact > Romp die drops it; ≤ steps down smallest (d4 gone). Romp acts last.

Four house cuts

  • Full-Force Hits: Never step down your Impact against a loser's bigger die. Win the Total, land full Impact.
  • Real Fallout Only: Starts at d6 minimum (no d4 PP-farming) and steps down by one in quiet recovery scenes. Past d12 drops out of the scene.
  • In-Pool Resources: Roll a Resource die inside your pool to keep +1 Total die, then expend until recovery.
  • Side Chars (Session 0): Shared Diamonds hold 2–3 d8 traits; swap up to 2 and absorb fitting Fallout.

Prime Set 1: Distinctions

Write three d8 Distinctions titled after your world's Identity Prompts (Texture, Foothold, Hook).

Universal Hinder rule: Roll any Distinction as d4 instead of d8 to gain 1 PP.

Cortex Dial Compiler

Match three Dials for Engine Mod, Prime Set 2 and Prime Set 3. Undertow (4th die): gain 1 shared d8 Side Char or Special, OR apply its Engine Mod in contrast scenes.

Palette feeds Engine Mod

  1. 1

    Prismatic (Bleed): When the uncanny bleeds into a scene, 1s & 2s both count as Openings; spend 1 PP to rename any Asset or Fallout in play.

  2. 2

    Neon (Burnout): Created Assets expire after 1 roll (1 PP sustains for the scene). Lean In: roll your own Fallout, then step it up.

  3. 3

    Chrome (Spec Sheet): Two d8 Signature Assets (Gear Limit: shut down for the scene → +1 PP).

  4. 4

    Dark (Doom Pool): Escalating d8, d6, d4 GM pool replaces static difficulty; grows on player Openings, spends dice for twists/Romps.

  5. 5

    Sepia (Living Serial): Log Session Titles; 1/session invoke for a free 1 PP effect or Flashback; cross off between sessions to step up a trait or add a d8 Signature Asset.

  6. 6

    Bright (Open Table): No GM Bank (GM PP spends go straight to targeted PCs) + allies share created Assets for free.

  7. 7

    Pastel (Gentle Stakes): PC Fallout only ever lands at d6 (or steps up existing Fallout by one), and steps down automatically in quiet scenes.

  8. 8

    Rust (Scarcity): Two 2d6 Salvage Resources (+1 Total die, expend). Stricken: Fallout > attached trait restricts you to keeping 1 Total die.

Setting feeds Prime Set 2

  1. 1

    Threshold: Attributes (d10, d8, d6: Pulse, Pattern, Poise) + one d8 Ability (strange gift or oddity w/ 1 Special & Limit: shut down for scene → +1 PP).

  2. 2

    Future: Roles (d10, 2×d8, d6, d4): Hacker, Fixer, Operator, Tech, Face.

  3. 3

    Space: Affiliations: Arenas (d10, d8, d6: Void, Station, Worlds) + shared d8 Vehicle (Engines, Frame, Systems).

  4. 4

    Modern: Roles (d10, d8, d6: Mouth, Mind, Muscle) + one Talent (unrated narrative Special).

  5. 5

    Industrial: Skills (Pyramid d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, rest d4: Brawl, Craft, Delve, Doctor, Prowl, Rig, Survey, Sway).

  6. 6

    Mythic: Attributes (d10, d8, d6: Might, Grace, Lore) + Power Set (two d8 mythic Powers + Limit).

  7. 7

    Hearth: Skills & Specialties (d10, 2×d8, 3×d6, rest d4: Cook, Grow, Mend, Notice, Parley, Tinker, Travel, Ward) + two d6 Specialties.

  8. 8

    Primal: Affiliations: Arenas (d10, d8, d6: Camp, Wilds, Lair) + d8 Beast Side Char or Signature Asset.

Tone feeds Prime Set 3

  1. 1

    Weird: Values (d10, d8, 2×d6: Order, Truth, Wonder, Self) w/ Statements (Question a belief to triple die for one roll, then step down & rewrite).

  2. 2

    Punk: Relationships: Factions (d10, d8, d6 across 3 dominant Blocs) w/ Statements (Question your stance to triple die & burn standing).

  3. 3

    Adventure: Values (d10, d8, 2×d6, d4: Discovery, Duty, Fortune, Glory, Loyalty) + 3 Goals (d6, d8, d10: roll & cross off in opposition, or check off when achieved to step up a trait).

  4. 4

    Noir: Relationships (d10, 2×d8, rest d6 toward PCs, informants & suspects) w/ Statements + GMC Vices (GMCs oppose using Greed, Control, Wrath).

  5. 5

    Pulp: Affiliations (d10, d8, d6: Solo, Duo, Team) + Hero Dice (win by 5+ to bank foe's top-rolling die; spend 1 PP later to roll & add to Total).

  6. 6

    Heroic: Values (d10, d8, 2×d6: Courage, Justice, Mercy, Truth) + Moral Support (step down your Value for the session to step up an ally's).

  7. 7

    Cozy: Relationships (d10, 2×d8, rest d6 toward PCs & neighbors) + Team Up (lend your Relationship die into an ally's pool risk-free).

  8. 8

    Peril: Values (d10, d8, 2×d6: Caution, Hope, Kin, Survival) + Trauma (Fallout past d12 leaves persistent d6 Trauma requiring recovery rolls).
